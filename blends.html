Chardonnay

Sauvignon Blanc

The Vibe:

Brisa Bay

A Chardonnay that feels like a breath of fresh air. The nose brings waves of ripe white peach, crisp apple, and cool stone aromas. In the mouth, it unfurls bright, energetic notes of lemon, mandarin, and clementine with an appealingly tender texture. It wraps up with a mouthwatering acidity and an exquisite sweet-tart balance that lingers through the finish, naturally inviting you to take another sip.

Quick Notes:

Profile: Ripe peach, bright citrus, and a smooth, tender finish.

Best Served: Cold, during a long lunch that rolls right into dinner.

The Vibe:

Brisa Bay

Our very first release of Sauvignon Blanc is a fresh, citrusy take on the Brisa Bay experience. Designed to be bright and energetic yet soft and easygoing, this is the wine that practically invites a friend to join you for a glass and a chat. Aromas of guava, passionfruit, and lush tropical flowers swirl from the glass, followed by juicy pineapple, lime, tangerine, and a cool vein of stony minerality that lingers through a mouthwatering finish.

Quick Notes:

Profile: Tropical fruit, juicy lime, and a crisp vein of stony minerality.

Best Served: Chilled, the second the sun comes out and the music starts.
