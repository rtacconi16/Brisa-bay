Brisa Bay in Atlanta

Atlanta is Brisa Bay’s deepest pocket in the South — nine shops, restaurants and clubs from Midtown to the perimeter, where a Napa bottle sits next to Georgia produce rather than behind a velvet rope. Look for us at independent grocers and rooms that pour by the glass when the weather finally breaks.
